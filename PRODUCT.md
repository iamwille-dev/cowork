# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product purpose

Cowork gives teams a shared place to talk with an AI coworker, delegate tasks, review artifacts, and keep track of reminders.

## Operating context

The public homepage introduces Cowork and its agent roles. The workspace includes conversations, tasks, reminders, and settings. Guests can join an invited conversation.

## Brand commitments

The product is named Cowork. The public homepage and workspace receive equal emphasis in this redesign. Apple is a reference for restraint, clarity, and interaction quality.
